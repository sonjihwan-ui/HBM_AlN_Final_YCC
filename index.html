TSV electrical-isolation node · universal 360° AlN collar
Every occupied TSV receives an annular AlN collar through the recessed segment.

Process assumption used in the final model: SiO₂ liner recess is not TSV-selective. The same AlN-depth segment is recessed around all occupied TSVs, then every TSV receives a full-circumference AlN collar. Ribs may connect to a collar or leave it isolated; below the AlN segment the original SiO₂ liner remains. The 4.5 µm TSV diameter is treated as an effective conductive Cu/barrier envelope because barrier thickness is not separately resolved.

Source / assumption boundary

Literature/reference basis

pre-stack target stage; Rao surrogate front/back layer thickness; Bank 1.5 W + TSV zone 0.5 W; 45 °C ambient; Si/Cu/oxide thermal properties; all-TSV common-depth liner recess with 360° AlN collar nodes in the modified die.

User-measured / project proxy

DOE / surrogate assumptions

TSV occupancy, exact TSV shape/shift variants, AlN reinforcement geometry, AlN volume fraction, effective bond layer and package thermal resistance. Aggregate front/back residual-stress values are intentionally not used.
